# Publishing, policy, moderation and commerce

## Always refresh these sources

- [Marketplace policy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-policy)
- [Publish to Marketplace](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/publish-to-marketplace)
- [Fees and commissions](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/marketplace-fees-and-commissions)
- [Categories](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/categories)
- [Intellectual property](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/intellectual-property)
- [Moderation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/moderation)

Keep eligibility, subscription/verification alternatives, 2SV, fees, price floors, publishing advances, quotas and beta access out of timeless instructions. Use the account/group's actual current eligibility and the displayed transaction terms. Older “apply to the UGC program first” and “Premium + ID only” tutorials may no longer describe current admission rules.

## Prepare the item

Confirm creator/account/group ownership and permissions, all input rights and the intended category. Complete actual Studio validation and visual tests. Assemble only the documented components; remove unrelated scripts, placeholder objects and unwanted accessories. Classic clothing, bodies, makeup and animation packs have their own submission paths.

Prepare a clear original name, description and faithful thumbnail. Review asset/thumbnail mutability before uploading: ordinary Marketplace asset content and thumbnails can require a new submission to fix, while some metadata is editable. Save evidence before paying for a submission.

Select creator, content type and category in the current Studio/Dashboard workflow. Review each original validation message, then submit under the user's existing authorization and record the asset/bundle ID and transaction result. Do not retry a paid submission blindly after an uncertain response; inspect whether it was already created.

## Policy review

Assess the actual design against current general and category rules. Examples requiring careful review include full-body accessories, floating head attachments, near-copies of Roblox/Limited items, obscured/invisible body parts, miscategorization, prohibited sexual content, and bodies with bundled clothing/accessories. Modesty layers and their coverage are specific body requirements.

Ownership of an uploaded asset does not establish ownership of copied art/brands/characters. Retain input provenance and licenses; avoid assuming downloaded or AI-generated content is automatically cleared. Offer an original revision when the intended work lacks demonstrated rights. Community tutorials cannot override current policy.

## After upload

Upload success, moderation approval and an item being on sale are separate states. Follow the asset's actual moderation result in Creator Dashboard. For a rejection, preserve the reason, identify the relevant rule, correct a working copy and use the supported appeal path where appropriate. Do not claim approval from a successful offline or Studio technical check.

Once approved and eligible, configure price, distribution, quantity/availability and allowed sale locations. Differentiate non-Limited versus Limited, paid versus free Limited issuance, publishing advance versus upload charge, and minimum selling price versus a fee. Some charges/thresholds vary by market or time. Resale support is not the same as trading support.

Use current commission rules for Marketplace versus in-experience purchases, including creator/affiliate shares, progressive commission behavior, restrictions and payouts. Do not calculate financial results from an obsolete fixed percentage. For regional/timed pricing and Limited behavior, retrieve those exact sections before recommending a configuration.

## Homestores and operations

The [Homestore](https://create.roblox.com/docs/marketplace/homestore) route and Avatar Editor/Marketplace APIs support showcasing/trying/purchasing items in an experience. Follow sale-location and ownership requirements; mock purchase prompts do not demonstrate an actual sale. Use Dashboard sales analytics for observed performance and the current policy for promotion, advertising and eligibility programs such as Today's Picks.

DevEx has separate eligibility, earned-Robux, tax and payout requirements. Read the linked official Help Center terms before advising about conversion or eligibility; asset revenue does not guarantee immediate DevEx eligibility.

Final publication evidence should identify the actual asset, creator, moderation state, sale state, checked time and remaining action. The plugin does not publish any Roblox asset merely by being installed.
